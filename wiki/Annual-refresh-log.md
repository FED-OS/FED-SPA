## 2026.2 — 2026-09-11

**What shipped**

- The licensed list grew from 1 to **47 records**: 34 GREEN/BLUE-swept establishments + 10 YELLOW-with-Clear-license establishments (predecessor caveats carried in notes) + 3 DOH license records promoted from evidence rows (they had a verified active license and an Address of Record but no separate directory listing). All statuses Clear; all addresses use the DOH Address of Record where the portal shows one.
- The watchlist tier went live with **44 entries**: 3 revoked, 30 no-license-found, 3 inactive, 8 expired/null-and-void. Every entry carries a documented portal search in its reason field — the "no license found" bar from the FAQ is met entry by entry.
- The envelope was re-encrypted for 2026.2 (44 entries). The committed blob is a workflow-test-password envelope; the maintainer re-encrypts with the production subscriber password before publishing (the only manual step left — see `docs/annual_update_workflow.md`).
- CSV export of the licensed list (`web/data/licensed.csv`, mirrored at `data/licensed.csv`) generated by the fan-out — the ROADMAP's most-requested "give me the data" item, delivered as a generated static file.
- All surface builds (web, extension, Android, Android Auto, watch, iOS) regenerated from the single source; the repo-root `data/` GitHub-Pages mirror joined the fan-out destinations (it had silently drifted to the seed record).

**Coverage at release:** 5 counties (St. Lucie 16 · Martin 1 · Palm Beach 26 · Broward 3 · Miami-Dade 1 licensed records), 112 municipalities listed along the corridor.

**What we learned doing it**

- The sweep's working-color taxonomy (GREEN/BLUE/YELLOW/RED/GRAY) maps cleanly onto the two data tiers, but the boundary cases are where the care lives: YELLOW rows split three ways (Clear license → licensed with caveats; no-match/closed → watchlist; not-checked → excluded), and evidence rows are documentation *unless* they carry a verified active license with an Address of Record. The full mapping is documented in `admin_scripts/xlsx_to_fedspa.py` and [County-sweep-2026.2.md](County-sweep-2026.2.md).
- MQA Addresses of Record come in more shapes than the converter's first draft assumed: suites in their own comma part ("131 North 2nd St, Ste# 220-222, Fort Pierce, FL 34950"), all-caps portal spellings (FT LAUDERDALE), and superseded municipality names (LAKE WORTH → Lake Worth Beach, renamed 2019). Parse from the tail, normalize to official spellings, never guess.
- 283 of 372 establishment rows are still unverified — that is the honest number. They stay out of both tiers until someone checks them; the directory is smaller and correct rather than bigger and wrong.

**Deferred to next cycle**

- The unverified 257: the sweep's checked-row pace was ~115 in one pass; a second pass on the same corridor plus interior counties (Wellington, Coral Springs, Miami interior) is the natural next cut.
- Re-verify licenses expiring 2027-08-31 (the seed record among them) — they age into the "expiring soon" filter before the next annual release.
- Production re-encryption of the envelope (the committed one uses a workflow-test password) and subscriber notification per the unlock-code rotation policy.
- Re-check the 44 watchlist entries whose last_known licenses may renew (a renewal moves them out of the watchlist; the reason field documents what to re-search).

## 2026.1 — 2026-09-07

**What shipped**

- The public licensed list (`data/public/licensed.json`) with the seed record: Halo Asian Spa, Inc — license MM41109, status Clear, issued 2020-12-14, expires 2027-08-31, 975 West Gateway Blvd Suite 105, Boynton Beach FL 33426. Discipline on file: No. Public complaint: No. Data as of 2026-09-07.
- The encrypted watchlist envelope (`data/private/unlicensed.encrypted.json`), v1, PBKDF2-SHA256 @ 310,000 iterations, AES-256-GCM. Records: 0 — a deliberate empty placeholder so the subscriber unlock path is exercised end-to-end on every surface without exposing any business to an empty-tier bug.
- All six surface builds (web, extension, Android, Android Auto, watch, iOS) generated from the single source via `admin_scripts/generate_public_files.py`.

**Coverage at release:** 1 licensed record, Palm Beach County only.

**What we learned doing it**

- The MQA portal renders name/address search well but the establishment (MM) search space is large; manual per-record verification is the only method that matches our constraints. The workflow is documented in [docs/annual_update_workflow.md](../docs/annual_update_workflow.md) and it takes minutes per record, not seconds — which is the real reason the cadence is annual.
- License numbers are stable; names and statuses change. Every record carries `last_checked` so a reader can judge freshness themselves.

**Deferred to next cycle**

- Broaden coverage beyond Palm Beach County. Priorities tracked as feature requests, not promises.
- First watchlist entries. The bar for entry on the watchlist is deliberately high: a documented search (what terms, what date, zero matches) recorded in `status_note`. See the [FAQ](FAQ.md) on why "no license found" is not "unlicensed."
- Re-verify the seed record's 2027-08-31 expiry — it lands inside the 2027 window, so it appears in the "expiring soon" filter before the next release unless an interim correction lands first.

## How to add a release row

1. Run the annual workflow (`docs/annual_update_workflow.md`) start to finish — including the verify step that decrypts the shipped envelope with the correct code.
2. Bump `version` in both `data/public/licensed.json` and the unlicensed plaintext source before encrypting (e.g. `2027.1`).
3. Append a row to the table above with shipped date and counts.
4. Append the machine-readable entry to `data/meta/changelog.md`.
5. Tag the commit `data-<year>.<n>` (e.g. `data-2026.2`) per the workflow's ship step.
