      How verification works

      Every entry is checked against the Florida Department of Health MQA verification portal - the same database the state runs at mqa-internet.doh.state.fl.us. We record license status, expiration, discipline flags and complaints, then re-verify the entire list once per year. You can always double-check any license yourself on the state portal - it's public information.

      A valid state license is separate from local county/city business tax receipts, which some municipalities also require.
